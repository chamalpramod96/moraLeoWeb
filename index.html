ABOUT US

Where Passion Meets Purpose.

Every great story begins with a purpose. Ours begins with service. The Leo Club of Moratuwa is more than a youth organization. It is a family of passionate young leaders united by a shared commitment to serve, lead, and inspire. Every project we undertake, every hand we extend, and every life we touch becomes another chapter in our journey of creating meaningful change. Together, we believe that true leadership is not measured by titles, but by the positive impact we leave on the lives of others.

Mission: To inspire and empower young leaders through meaningful service, foster fellowship and personal growth, and create lasting positive change with compassion, integrity, and dedication.

Vision: To build a future where every act of kindness inspires hope, every leader serves with purpose, and every community grows stronger through selfless service.

OUR PROJECTS

Where Ideas Become Impact

Aloka 26

A spiritual beginning to the new Leoistic year, where members gathered at Bellanwila Rajamaha Viharaya to seek blessings, unity, and inspiration for another year of meaningful service.

Tri Life 26 — Phase 01, Day 01

Through the Paws of Hope initiative, our members shared nutritious meals with stray dogs in Rawathawaththa, proving that even the smallest act of kindness can make a lasting difference.

Leo Voyage

A collaborative orientation programme with the Leo Club of Prince of Wales College, inspiring aspiring Leos to begin their leadership journey with confidence, purpose, and service.

Saviya

An entrepreneurship programme designed to empower unemployed and low income families with the knowledge and practical skills needed to build sustainable livelihoods.

Leo Ape Dansala

The first Leo Ape Dansala of the new Leoistic year began with an ice cream dansala on July Poya, spreading joy, kindness, and smiles while continuing a cherished tradition of community service.
